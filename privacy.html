Salo AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how the Salo AI nutrition and workout tracker ("the App"), developed by Roman Shtykalo ("I", "me"), handles your information. I am the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The App is for adults (18+). It is not a medical service and gives no medical advice.

1. Data you enter

The App stores your profile (goal, sex, birth date, height, current and target weight, activity level, diet type, breastfeeding status, weekly workout goal, allergies, language and units) and what you log: meals and foods, your own foods and recipes (with photos you attach to them), favorite foods, water, weight, body measurements, workouts, habits and the days you tick them, and steps and bed and wake times you type in yourself, and the questions you ask the coach. This data is stored on my server so that it syncs between your devices and the App can compute your targets and analyses. It is linked to your account. I do not sell it, show ads with it, or use it to train AI models.

Food, body, workout, sleep and breastfeeding entries can be health data. They are stored only because you choose to enter them, after you accept the consent text in the App.

2. Account (Google and Apple sign-in)

You sign in with Google or Apple. The App receives an account identifier from them; it does not receive your password, and I do not store your name or email address. If you delete your account, the App also asks Apple to revoke its sign-in.

3. Logging with AI

When you describe a meal in text or voice, or take a photo of a meal or a nutrition label, that text or photo is sent to the AI provider Anthropic (Claude), which returns the foods and amounts. Questions to the coach are sent together with a summary of your profile and recent log. Voice notes are first turned into text on my server; the audio is not kept. Photos sent for logging and audio are not stored on my server after they are processed. Photos you attach to your own recipes are stored with your account until you remove them, delete the recipe or delete your account. Anthropic processes this data on my instructions and may do so in the United States. See the Anthropic privacy policy. AI results are estimates: check them before saving.

To avoid asking the AI the same thing twice, the server keeps a shared list of short food phrases and the food each one means (for example "2 eggs" means boiled eggs). It contains no account identifier and none of your own foods.

4. Apple Health and Health Connect

If you turn the connection on, the App reads steps, active energy, sleep, workouts and weight from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) to show them next to your log and to let the coach answer questions about them, and it can save the food, water and weight you log there. You can turn this off in the App or in the system settings at any time. Data read from these sources is not used for advertising and is not shared with anyone else.

5. Barcode lookup

When you scan a product barcode, the server looks the number up in Open Food Facts and in the US Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central. Only the barcode number is sent.

6. Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes. A crash report contains a stack trace, app state, device and app information (model, OS version, app version, free memory) and a Crashlytics installation identifier. Crash reports never include what you log. They are retained for 90 days. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

7. Usage steps

To see where people stop during setup, the App records a few named steps on my server, such as "profile completed" or "first meal logged", with a random install number. They contain nothing you log. The App has no ads, does not use the advertising ID, does not track you across other apps or sites, and uses no third-party analytics.

8. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract: storing and syncing your data, and logging with AI, to provide the App.
  • Explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)): health-related entries and data from Apple Health or Health Connect. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting the entries or your account.
  • Legitimate interest: crash reporting, keeping the service secure, and usage steps that contain none of your entries.

9. International transfers

Anthropic and Google (Firebase) may process data outside the EU/EEA and Ukraine, including in the United States, under Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

10. Retention and deletion

Your account and log are kept until you delete them. In the App you can edit or delete entries, and delete your account (Settings → Delete account), which removes your account and all your data at once. Coach messages are kept for 90 days. Settings → Export my data gives you a copy of everything held about you.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Most of these you can exercise directly in the App (edit or delete entries, export your data, delete your account). For anything else, contact me at the address below; I answer within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

12. Children

The App is not for anyone under 18. I do not knowingly collect data of minors; if you believe a minor uses the App, write to me and I will delete the account.

13. Changes

I may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date, and the App asks you to accept a changed consent text again.
